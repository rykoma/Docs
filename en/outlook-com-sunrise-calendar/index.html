Viewing your Outlook.com calendar in Sunrise Calendar

Note

Sunrise Calendar, the app described in this article, shut down in 2016. This article is kept as a historical record of the information at the time it was written, and the steps described here can no longer be reproduced.

This is a story about my Android phone. I use the Outlook.com calendar to manage my schedule. I used the Outlook.com app to sync with my device, and viewed the calendar in Jorte.

But recently I started getting frequent notifications telling me to switch to Outlook. It seemed like a good idea to follow along, so I switched to Outlook. However, syncing with my device stopped working, and the calendar also stopped showing up in Jorte.

While wondering what to do, I thought of trying Sunrise, which Microsoft had recently acquired. After experimenting with it a bit, I found the steps were simple, just three steps.

  1. On the Android device, add the Outlook.com account as an ActiveSync account.
  2. Install Sunrise Calendar.
  3. In Sunrise Calendar, add the Outlook.com account as an Exchange account.

So, adding an account to Sunrise Calendar requires an ActiveSync account, and the Outlook.com account can be added to an Android device as an ActiveSync account. This is how I got Sunrise Calendar to show my Outlook.com calendar.
