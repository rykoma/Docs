Choose one of these two ways to contact me.

## Reporting an error or suggesting an improvement

If you find a typo, a factual error, a broken link, or have another suggestion for an article, please let me know through the GitHub issue form below. Including the title or URL of the article helps me respond faster. This requires a GitHub account.

[Create an issue (GitHub)](https://github.com/rykoma/Docs/issues/new?template=blog-feedback-en.yml)

## Anything else

For anything else, such as a professional inquiry, please reach out to me on LinkedIn. This requires a LinkedIn account.

[LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rykoma/)
